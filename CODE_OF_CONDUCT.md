# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Núcleo AI project aims to keep technical discussion respectful, practical, and safe. Anyone participating in Issues, discussions, invited reviews, or other project spaces is expected to act professionally.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on the issue, implementation, or evidence rather than a person.
- State uncertainty clearly and accept good-faith corrections.
- Respect privacy, consent, copyright, licensing, and security boundaries.
- Share only material you are authorized to disclose.
- Keep discussion relevant and avoid harassment, insults, threats, discrimination, or deliberate disruption.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, doxxing, hateful conduct, persistent personal attacks, malicious disclosure of private data, and knowingly posting credentials or exploit details in public channels are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the GitHub profile linked from this repository. The maintainer may edit, hide, or close content; decline contributions; or restrict participation when necessary to protect people and the project.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces maintained by the repository owner. It is not a promise of a particular enforcement outcome or response time.
